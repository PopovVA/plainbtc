If you are comparing crypto exchanges, you may see a Gemini “refer a friend” or Recruit offer. This guide explains how that program works using Gemini’s own published terms, not a screenshot of someone else’s signup page. Offers change. Always read the terms and the offer shown to you when you sign up.

This page includes PlainBTC’s [Gemini referral link](LREF). If you create a new account through that link and meet Gemini’s current terms, PlainBTC may earn a referral reward at no extra cost to you. That is a material connection, and it does not change the official figures quoted below.

!takeaways
- Gemini’s official [Referral Program Terms](L1) (last stated as updated January 20, 2026) describe the Recruit offer that has been in market since November 24, 2025.
- In the United States, Hong Kong, and New Zealand, the terms list a <strong>US$50</strong> reward for each of the referrer and the new user, after identity verification and a qualifying trade of at least <strong>US$100</strong> within <strong>30 days</strong> of signup. Gemini says the reward is paid <strong>32 days</strong> after signup.
- Those same terms list a referrer cap of <strong>US$1,500 / €1,500 / A$1,500</strong>, depending on the currency of the offer. Do not assume every row in that table is still open for new accounts.
- Effective <strong>6 April 2026</strong>, Gemini closed all customer accounts in the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area, and Australia, according to Gemini’s [areas of availability](L2) page and its [Gemini 2.0 blog post](L3) (February 5, 2026). Those markets are not a current signup path.

## What the official terms say

Gemini calls the current consumer referral offer <strong>Recruit</strong>. The legal page is [gemini.com/legal/referral-program-terms](L1). The terms we reviewed were last stated as updated on January 20, 2026, and they describe an offer in market from November 24, 2025.

For the United States, Hong Kong, and New Zealand, the table on that page lists:

1. A reward of <strong>US$50</strong> for the referrer and <strong>US$50</strong> for the referred user.
2. The new user must complete identity verification.
3. The new user must complete a qualifying trade of at least <strong>US$100</strong> within <strong>30 days</strong> of opening the account.
4. Gemini says it pays the reward <strong>32 days after signup</strong>.

The same terms also list an older or regional euro-denominated figure (including €20) for some European markets. That row is historical context only for this guide: those markets were later closed, as described below. Do not sign up expecting a European Gemini account or a euro reward in 2026 unless Gemini republishes terms that say otherwise.

## Timing, caps, and other conditions

A few conditions on the official terms page are easy to miss:

- The <strong>$100</strong> qualifying trade has to happen inside the <strong>30-day</strong> window after signup. A later trade may not count.
- Payment is described as <strong>32 days after signup</strong>, not instantly after the trade.
- A referrer cap is listed as <strong>US$1,500</strong>, <strong>€1,500</strong>, or <strong>A$1,500</strong>, depending on the currency of the offer. Once you hit the cap, later referrals may not pay.
- The new account has to be created through the referral link. Signing up without the link, or with an account that already exists, will not qualify.
- Gemini can change or end the program. The numbers on this page are a reading of the published terms as of the date in the sources, not a promise that the same offer will appear on your screen tomorrow.

## Where Gemini is — and is not — available

Availability is separate from the reward table. Gemini’s [areas of availability](L2) page is the place to check whether you can open or keep an account.

Effective <strong>6 April 2026</strong>, Gemini closed all customer accounts in the <strong>United Kingdom</strong>, the <strong>European Economic Area</strong>, and <strong>Australia</strong>. Gemini announced that change in its [Gemini 2.0 post](L3) on February 5, 2026, and the availability page still states the closure. Today is after that date, so those markets are closed, not “closing soon.”

The availability page still lists the United States (all states, in the version we checked), some Latin American countries (including Brazil and Chile), Singapore, Israel, Turkey, and parts of Africa, among others. The reward table’s mention of Hong Kong and New Zealand is not, by itself, proof that new accounts are open there on a given day. If you live in either place, confirm on Gemini’s availability page and on the signup screen before you rely on the $50 figure.

Spanish and Portuguese editions of this site do not carry a Gemini referral link, even though Brazil appears on Gemini’s availability list. The European Spanish and Portuguese audiences overlap with the EEA, which Gemini has closed.

## How to use a referral link, if Gemini is offered where you live

This is a process description, not a recommendation to open an account.

1. Confirm on Gemini’s [areas of availability](L2) page that Gemini is offered where you live.
2. Open the referral link in a browser you control and create a <strong>new</strong> Gemini account. If you already have an account, the referral will not apply.
3. Complete identity verification when Gemini asks for it.
4. If you still want to proceed, place a qualifying trade of at least the amount in the current terms (the published figure is <strong>US$100</strong>) within <strong>30 days</strong> of signup.
5. Keep records of the signup date. Gemini’s terms describe payment <strong>32 days after signup</strong>.
6. Read the live offer on the signup page. If it disagrees with this article, trust the live offer and the legal terms.

If Gemini is not available in your country, do not try to work around the restriction. Use a platform that is actually licensed or offered where you live, or do nothing.

## Common pitfalls

- <strong>Wrong country.</strong> A referral link does not reopen a closed market. UK, EEA, and Australian residents should not expect to create a new Gemini account after 6 April 2026.
- <strong>No verification or no trade.</strong> The published $50 figure is not paid for clicking a link. The terms require identity verification and a qualifying trade inside 30 days.
- <strong>Existing accounts.</strong> The program is for new users who sign up through the link.
- <strong>Assuming Hong Kong or New Zealand is open.</strong> Those names appear in the reward table. Confirm current availability before you count on the offer.
- <strong>Stale bonus amounts.</strong> Third-party blogs often repeat expired numbers. This page quotes the January 20, 2026 terms; check that page again if you are reading this later.

## FAQ

### How much is the Gemini referral bonus in 2026?

Gemini’s Referral Program Terms, last stated as updated January 20, 2026, list US$50 for each side in the United States, Hong Kong, and New Zealand, after identity verification and a US$100 qualifying trade within 30 days of signup. That is the official figure we are quoting. It can change; check the live terms.

### When does Gemini pay the referral bonus?

The same terms say the reward is paid 32 days after signup, not immediately after the trade.

### Is the Gemini referral bonus available in Europe or the UK?

Not as a current signup path. Gemini closed all customer accounts in the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area, and Australia effective 6 April 2026, according to Gemini’s official availability page and its February 5, 2026 Gemini 2.0 blog post.

### Do I have to use a referral link?

To be a referred user under the program, yes: the official terms require the new account to be created through the referrer’s link. You can also sign up without a referral link; you then would not be in this program.

### Is this financial advice?

No. This is an explanation of published program terms and availability. Cryptocurrency is volatile, and you can lose money. Whether you open an account is your decision.
