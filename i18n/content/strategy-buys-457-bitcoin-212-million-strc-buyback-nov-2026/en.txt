Strategy, the company that holds more bitcoin than any other public company, kept buying during last week's crash, but only a little. In a [Form 8-K filed on Monday](L1), the company said it bought <strong>457 bitcoin</strong> for about <strong>$29.6 million</strong> between Nov. 16 and Nov. 22, at an average price of $64,898 per coin, including fees.

That brings its holdings to <strong>851,305 BTC</strong>, bought for a total of about $64.23 billion, or an average of <strong>$75,446</strong> per bitcoin. The bigger story in the filing is where the rest of the money went: Strategy spent <strong>$211.8 million</strong> buying back its own STRC preferred shares, about seven times what it spent on bitcoin.

!takeaways
- Strategy bought 457 BTC for $29.6 million from Nov. 16 to 22, at an average of $64,898 per coin. It now holds 851,305 BTC.
- Its average cost is $75,446 per bitcoin, well above Monday's price of about $62,900 on Kraken.
- It repurchased 2,183,747 STRC preferred shares for $211.8 million and raised its preferred stock buyback program from $3 billion to $4 billion.
- The money came from selling about 1.93 million MSTR common shares for $176.7 million, plus $64.8 million of cash.

!figure:chart

## Where the money came from

Strategy raised <strong>$176.7 million</strong> last week by selling 1,925,676 shares of its Class A common stock (ticker MSTR) through an at-the-market program, which lets a company sell new shares gradually into the market. It sold none of its four preferred stocks.

Of that $176.7 million, $29.6 million paid for the bitcoin. The remaining $147.0 million went to buying back STRC shares. Strategy used another $64.8 million from its "USD Cash" balance for more STRC buybacks, which together came to $211.8 million. It also paid $17.6 million of preferred dividends from a separate dollar reserve.

## Why buy back STRC?

STRC, also called Stretch, is a preferred stock with a variable dividend, currently at a 12% annual rate, according to [The Block](L3). Strategy sold it at $100 a share to raise money for bitcoin. Recently, it has been trading below that price.

On a livestream on Friday, executive chairman Michael Saylor described the market as a credit contraction, The Block reported. He said natural demand for STRC at a 12% rate is likely between $5 billion and $7.5 billion, and that Strategy is shrinking the amount of STRC outstanding until it reaches a level the market supports. CEO Phong Le said the company has bought back about $3 billion of preferred stock at prices between $95 and $99.

The board on Monday raised the buyback program for these preferred shares from $3.0 billion to <strong>$4.0 billion</strong>. After the increase, $1.12 billion is still available, according to the [company's announcement](L2). The board also let management move up to $1.0 billion from its dividend reserve to its general cash pile. After that, the reserve held $3.73 billion and the cash balance $1.09 billion.

## Bitcoin is below Strategy's cost

Bitcoin fell as low as $55,100 on Kraken early Saturday, its lowest level since September 2024, before bouncing to about <strong>$62,900</strong> on Monday morning, according to Kraken's [public price data](L4). At that price, Strategy's 851,305 bitcoin are worth about $53.5 billion, roughly $10.7 billion less than the company paid for them.

Saylor had hinted at the purchase on Sunday with a post saying "The dots aren't done," The Block noted. Last week's buy is smaller than the 485 BTC Strategy reported a week earlier.

## What it means for beginners

Strategy is a stock, not a bitcoin fund. Its share price moves with bitcoin, but also with how much new stock it sells, how much debt and preferred stock it carries, and what investors think of its plan. Buying MSTR is not the same as owning bitcoin.

If you simply want bitcoin, you can buy a small amount directly on a large regulated exchange such as [Coinbase](REF), or own a spot bitcoin ETF in a brokerage account. Many beginners spread purchases over time instead of buying all at once. Only invest money you can afford to lose. This is not investment advice.

## What to watch next

- Whether Strategy keeps buying bitcoin next week, or spends more on STRC buybacks.
- STRC's price compared with its $100 issue price.
- How much of the $17.9 billion of MSTR stock still available under its sales program Strategy uses.
