PlainBTC is an independent informational site for U.S. readers who want clear crypto coverage without hype. We publish short market recaps, regulation explainers, and beginner how-tos—written so you can follow the news and the product steps without a trading desk dictionary.

We are not a broker, adviser, or exchange. Some articles include a Coinbase referral link so eligible new users can open an account; if you use it and complete a qualifying trade, we may earn a referral reward at no extra cost to you. Offer for new U.S. customers; not available in Washington state. Full details are on our [Disclosure](/disclosure) page.

Nothing on this site is financial, investment, legal, or tax advice. Cryptocurrency is volatile and you can lose money. Always verify steps and fees in primary sources (for example Coinbase Help) before acting.
