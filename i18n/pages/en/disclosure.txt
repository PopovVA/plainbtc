In accordance with the U.S. Federal Trade Commission’s endorsement guides, PlainBTC discloses material connections with advertisers and referral programs.

## Coinbase referral links

Some English pages on this site include a Coinbase referral link ([https://coinbase.com/join/F2XRWRL?src=referral-link](REFD)). If you create a <strong>new</strong> Coinbase account through that link and complete a qualifying trade, PlainBTC may receive a referral reward. Offer for new U.S. customers; not available in Washington state; terms on Coinbase’s site. We do not state bonus amounts here; always check the offer shown on Coinbase’s signup page and Coinbase’s current referral terms. Only our English pages contain this referral link; other language versions of PlainBTC do not. According to Coinbase’s official invite page (https://www.coinbase.com/invite), the offer is for new U.S. customers except Washington state, the referred friend’s first trade must meet Coinbase’s stated minimum, and a referrer can invite only a limited number of users. Those figures can change; rely on Coinbase’s current terms.

Using our link does not cost you extra. We are not affiliated with Coinbase beyond participation in Coinbase’s standard referral program.

## How we mark links

Referral links on this site use <code>rel="sponsored"</code> (and open in a new tab). Article pages also include an on-page disclosure next to the call-to-action.

## Not financial advice

Content on PlainBTC is for informational and educational purposes only. It is not investment, trading, legal, tax, or financial advice. Cryptocurrency is volatile and you can lose money. Do your own research and consider consulting a qualified professional before making financial decisions.

## Independence

Editorial descriptions of products and market events are our own. Compensation from referral programs does not change disclosed facts sourced from public reporting, but it is a material connection you should know about when evaluating our content.

## Newsletter and visit statistics

If you subscribe to our email newsletter, we store your email address, the language and page you signed up from, and the dates you signed up, confirmed and unsubscribed. We use it only to send you PlainBTC article updates and never sell or share it. It is processed by our database host (Supabase) and our email delivery provider solely to deliver those emails. We use double opt-in: nothing is sent until you confirm, and every email contains an unsubscribe link. After you unsubscribe we keep only a suppression record so you are not emailed again; to have your address erased completely, contact us using the details on the About page.

Separately, our visit statistics are cookieless: we do not store IP addresses, only a daily-rotating anonymous hash and the visitor’s country.
