Trump hosts second memecoin dinner as the TRUMP token trades near record lows

Published Nov 22, 2026 · PlainBTC · United States · ~5 min
Share

President Donald Trump is set to host a dinner on Sunday night, Nov. 22, for the biggest holders of his TRUMP memecoin at Trump National Golf Club outside Washington, D.C. According to the event's official website, the top 185 holders get a seat, and the top 29 get a VIP reception. Boxer Floyd Mayweather, "Shark Tank" investor Kevin O'Leary and former NBA player Tristan Thompson are listed as guests. The site says there will be no private meetings with the president.

The mood is very different from the first dinner in May 2025. TRUMP was trading at about $0.88 on Kraken on Sunday evening, according to PlainBTC's check of Kraken's public price data. Just after midnight Central time on Saturday, it set a record low of $0.798 on Kraken. The Block put the token's all-time high at $73.43 in January 2025, which means it has lost about 99% of its value.

Key takeaways

  • Trump hosts the top 185 TRUMP memecoin holders at his golf club in Sterling, Virginia, on Sunday, Nov. 22. The top 29 get a VIP reception.
  • TRUMP trades near $0.88, close to a record low of $0.798 set on Kraken on Saturday and about 99% below its January 2025 peak.
  • Reuters says wallets on the public leaderboard held $8.6 million, versus $148 million for the first dinner in May 2025.
  • Democrats, who will control both chambers of Congress next year, say they plan to investigate the Trump family's crypto ventures.

How the contest worked

Seats were not sold. Instead, the token's promoter, Fight Fight Fight LLC, ranked wallets by their average TRUMP holdings between Sept. 30 and Nov. 16, the scoring window listed on the event site. The more tokens a wallet held, and the longer it held them, the higher it ranked. The top four VIP holders with at least 1 million TRUMP are promised 18-karat gold Trump watches.

Interest has dropped sharply. Reuters reported that the public leaderboard listed just 130 wallets when the contest closed, holding a combined $8.6 million, compared with $148 million for the first dinner. The Block, citing a Washington Post analysis, said one holder reached 179th place after buying only about $518 of TRUMP, while it took at least about $55,000 worth to qualify for the first dinner.

Who is going, and who is not

The Block reported that trading platform FastX, which topped the leaderboard, plans to attend "as an independent holder." Tron founder Justin Sun, the largest holder at the first dinner, is not attending, according to The Wall Street Journal, as cited by The Block. Reuters said a review by the consumer group Public Citizen found that more than half of the leaderboard wallets were tied to exchanges that do not serve U.S. users.

Political scrutiny

Eight Democratic senators led by Sen. Adam Schiff wrote to the White House last month, saying the scoring system appeared "carefully constructed to increase investment in $TRUMP," NBC News reported. Democrats won control of both chambers of Congress in this month's midterms and have said they will look into the president's crypto ventures. The White House referred questions to Fight Fight Fight. A White House spokesperson previously told Reuters that neither the president nor his family "has ever engaged" in conflicts of interest.

Reuters reported that the memecoin has earned the Trump family at least $635 million, according to the president's financial disclosure.

What it means for beginners

A memecoin is a token built mostly on attention, a brand or a joke, not on a business that earns money. TRUMP shows how quickly that attention can fade: a buyer at the first dinner's price would now be down more than 90%. Contests that reward you for holding a token are a marketing tool, not a sign the token will gain value. If you want to own crypto, many beginners start with bitcoin bought in small amounts on a large regulated exchange such as Coinbase. Only invest what you can afford to lose. This is not investment advice.

What to watch next

  • Any official statement from the organizers or the White House after the dinner.
  • Whether TRUMP holds above Saturday's record low of $0.798 on Kraken.
  • The promoter's next contest, a Super Bowl package for the top 18 holders, which The Block says opens its scoring window on Sunday.
