Franklin Templeton's crypto index ETF will add Hyperliquid's HYPE on Dec. 1, its eighth coin next to bitcoin and ether

Published Nov 25, 2026 · PlainBTC · United States · ~5 min
Share

A small crypto fund run by the asset manager Franklin Templeton is about to get a new coin. The Franklin Crypto Index ETF, which trades under the ticker EZPZ, will add Hyperliquid (HYPE) to its holdings on or about Dec. 1, 2026, according to a prospectus supplement filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission on Wednesday, Nov. 25.

HYPE is the token of Hyperliquid, a blockchain built around a crypto trading platform best known for perpetual futures, a type of leveraged bet on prices. Once the change takes effect, the fund will hold eight cryptocurrencies: bitcoin, ether, XRP, Solana, Dogecoin, Cardano, Stellar Lumens and HYPE.

Key takeaways

  • Franklin Templeton's EZPZ fund will start holding HYPE on or about Dec. 1, 2026, a Nov. 25 SEC filing says.
  • The change follows its benchmark, the CF Institutional Digital Asset Index, which adds HYPE as its only new coin on Dec. 1.
  • EZPZ is small: about $17.02 million in net assets on Sept. 30, with 76.39% in bitcoin, according to its latest quarterly report.
  • The filing does not say how much HYPE the fund will hold. Weights follow the index, which is rebalanced every quarter.

Why the fund is adding HYPE

EZPZ does not pick coins on its own. It tries to track the CF Institutional Digital Asset Index, run by CF Benchmarks. In its reconstitution notice, CF Benchmarks said HYPE will be added to that index on Dec. 1, with no coins removed. The fund then buys the same coins "in approximately the same weights" as the index, the filing says.

A new token can join quickly because of SEC-approved generic listing standards adopted by the Cboe BZX exchange. The filing says those rules let the fund hold additional coins that are part of its index, without a separate approval for each one. The index stays on a quarterly rebalancing schedule, so coins can be added or removed again later.

What EZPZ holds today

The fund is tiny next to the big bitcoin ETFs. Its quarterly report shows net assets of about $17.02 million on Sept. 30, 2026. Bitcoin made up 76.39% of the fund, ether 12.76%, XRP 5.37% and Solana 3.76%. Dogecoin, Cardano and Stellar Lumens were each below 1%. A small slice of HYPE would sit alongside them, but the filing does not give a weight.

A big year for HYPE

HYPE has stood out in a weak market. The Block reported that the token was up more than 120% this year as of Nov. 24, which made it one of the 10 largest cryptocurrencies, with a market value of about $13.6 billion. On Kraken, HYPE's daily close peaked near $97 on Sept. 22 and was about $61.67 on Nov. 25.

HYPE already appears in other U.S. funds. The Block said three spot Hyperliquid ETFs have traded more than $3.3 billion since launching this year but hold less than $250 million combined. crypto.news noted that Bitwise's 10 Crypto Index ETF added HYPE in July.

What this means for beginners

An index ETF lets you own a basket of coins through a regular brokerage account, without managing wallets or keys. The trade-off is that you do not hold the coins yourself, you pay the fund's fees, and you get whatever the index decides to include, including newer and more volatile tokens like HYPE.

In a fund like EZPZ, bitcoin still drives most of the result, so a new small holding is unlikely to change much on day one. Still, HYPE has swung widely in 2026, and every coin in the basket can fall sharply. If you prefer to buy bitcoin directly, a regulated U.S. exchange such as Coinbase lets you start with small amounts.

What to watch next

  • How much HYPE EZPZ actually holds after the Dec. 1 rebalance.
  • Whether other index funds that follow similar benchmarks add HYPE too.
  • How HYPE trades as the year ends, after a pullback from its September high.
